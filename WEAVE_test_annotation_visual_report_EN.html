The test shows that a single episode can be described simultaneously at the levels of action, participants, ideology, language, and affect. The main value of the pilot lies not in tagging itself, but in the possibility of comparing configurations across texts and identifying points at which categories require interpretive negotiation.

1. Text as a System of Overlapping Layers

Toggle layers on and off. The same passage can perform several analytical functions at the same time.

That evening, in the square, a group of young men surrounded Samir. One of them shouted that “foreigners have no business being here”, then punched him in the face. The others laughed and egged the attacker on. Two police officers watched the incident from across the street but did not intervene. Samir later said that since then he has been afraid to leave the house in the evening.

This sequence demonstrates the usefulness of the structure: the right-wing character of violence does not have to be imposed as an external label. The annotation preserves information about the textual basis on which it was identified – here, through xenophobic exclusion and the construction of the “foreigner” as an outsider.

6. The Pilot Reveals Points Requiring Interpretive Negotiation

Test case: police observe the attack and do not intervene.
The annotation records structural + institutionalAction, while also assigning ambiguous. The annotation note itself states that calibration should determine whether non-intervention should be treated as institutionalAction.

This is a research-relevant result rather than a flaw in the scheme. The pilot helps distinguish categories that can be applied consistently from those that require clearer definitions, boundary examples, or should remain within the domain of close reading.

8. What This Single Test Already Shows – and What It Does Not Yet Show

It shows: that multi-valued features work correctly, that several analytical layers can overlap on the same passage, that mixed participant roles can be represented, and that local annotation can be combined with interpretive notes.

It does not yet show: representative tendencies, inter-annotator agreement, differences between periods, or the stability of all categories. These require a shared calibration sample followed by a larger body of material.
